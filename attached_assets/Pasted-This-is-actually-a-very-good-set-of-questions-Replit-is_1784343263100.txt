# The Three Questions I'd Add

These are the questions I think are *missing*, and they're arguably the most important.

## 1. Why wouldn't someone just use ChatGPT or Perplexity?

This is your biggest competitive question.

Your answer should be something like:

> Because ChatGPT and Perplexity answer questions when asked. Athlete Intelligence Agents continuously monitor, structure, verify, and proactively notify users of changes over time.

That difference should be reflected in every feature.

---

## 2. What proprietary asset are we building?

If your answer is:

"An AI app"

you're vulnerable.

If your answer is:

"A continuously updating, structured athlete knowledge graph with verified relationships and historical change tracking."

that's much stronger.

---

## 3. What happens after Athlete Intelligence?

I wouldn't mention this publicly, but internally I'd define the long-term vision:

> Build the Intelligence Platform once.

> Launch Athlete Intelligence first.

> Reuse the same platform to power Tourism Intelligence and future verticals.

That gives you a coherent product strategy without diluting the initial focus.

## One strategic adjustment

One answer I would *not* lock in yet is the choice of sport. Before deciding on cycling, triathlon, rugby, or another niche, I'd spend a week interviewing 10–15 potential users (scouts, performance directors, agents, or national sporting organisations) and ask about their current research workflow. The best launch market isn't necessarily the one with the least competition—it's the one where people have a painful, frequent problem and no tool they're genuinely happy with.

Everything else can be documented now, but I'd treat the initial sport selection as a hypothesis to validate rather than a fixed decision.

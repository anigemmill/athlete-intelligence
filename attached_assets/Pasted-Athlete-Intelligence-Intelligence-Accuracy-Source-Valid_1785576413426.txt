Athlete Intelligence – Intelligence Accuracy & Source Validation Sprint

The application has reached a point where the design system, dashboard, Health Panel, and supporting infrastructure are in a good place.

The highest priority is now intelligence quality.

The platform is still returning stale, incomplete or incorrect information.

This sprint is not about adding new features.

It is about making the intelligence trustworthy.

Objective

Determine why Athlete Intelligence is not consistently retrieving the latest, most accurate publicly available information.

Do not assume the issue is the crawler.

Trace the entire intelligence pipeline from discovery to display.

I want evidence, not assumptions.

Objective 1 – Full Pipeline Audit

Select 10 athletes from different sports and different levels (high school, national, international).

For each athlete, trace every stage of the pipeline.

Show:

Discovery
Search queries generated
External sources queried
Raw responses
AI extraction
Validation
Database writes
Data shown in the UI

For every incorrect value, identify exactly where it entered the pipeline.

Objective 2 – Accuracy Audit

Compare the stored data against current publicly available information.

Audit:

Athlete photo
Biography
Current team
Coach
Manager
Sponsors
Results
Rankings
Competitions
Timeline
Social accounts
Social follower counts
Awards
National representation

For every incorrect field explain:

Current value
Correct value
Source used
Better source (if available)
Root cause

Do not guess.

Objective 3 – Source Quality Audit

For every data category identify the source hierarchy.

For example:

Results

Official federation results
Official event websites
National governing bodies
Trusted timing providers
Reputable media
AI extraction

The platform should always prefer the highest-authority source available.

Produce the source hierarchy currently being used and recommend improvements.

Objective 4 – Data Freshness

Explain why stale information exists.

Investigate:

Crawl frequency
Cache invalidation
Failed refreshes
Skipped athletes
Scheduler behaviour
Prompt wording
Search queries
API limitations

Determine why examples such as:

February competitions
Old results
Outdated photos
Old rankings

are still appearing.

Objective 5 – Photo Retrieval

Investigate why athlete photos are often old or incorrect.

Explain:

Current image source
Selection algorithm
Refresh strategy
Confidence strategy
Why better images are not replacing older ones

Recommend a better approach if required.

Objective 6 – Intelligence Quality Score

Create an internal quality score for every athlete.

Example:

Profile completeness
Freshness
Source diversity
Evidence quality
Timeline completeness
Results completeness
Photo quality
Confidence

Produce an overall Intelligence Quality score out of 100.

Objective 7 – Root Cause Report

After tracing the complete pipeline, identify the five biggest causes of inaccurate intelligence.

Rank them by impact.

For each issue include:

Root cause
User impact
Recommended solution
Estimated implementation effort
Objective 8 – Improvement Plan

Based on the investigation, produce a prioritised roadmap for improving intelligence quality.

Prioritise changes that will have the greatest impact on accuracy before adding new functionality.

Do not implement these recommendations yet.

Wait for approval.

Deliverables

Provide:

1. Pipeline Trace

A complete end-to-end trace for all sampled athletes.

2. Accuracy Report

Every incorrect field identified.

3. Source Audit

Current source hierarchy and recommendations.

4. Freshness Report

Why stale information is occurring.

5. Photo Audit

Current image retrieval strategy and recommendations.

6. Intelligence Quality Score

Score each athlete and explain deductions.

7. Root Cause Analysis

Top five issues ranked by impact.

8. Recommended Roadmap

Prioritised improvements to maximise intelligence quality.

Important

Do not assume the AI model is the problem.

The issue may lie in:

search query generation,
source selection,
source prioritisation,
validation,
evidence handling,
caching,
refresh scheduling,
database writes,
display logic,
or a combination of these.

Trace the evidence before proposing solutions.

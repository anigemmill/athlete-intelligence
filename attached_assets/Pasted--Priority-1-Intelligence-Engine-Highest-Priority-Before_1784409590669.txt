🥇 Priority 1 — Intelligence Engine (Highest Priority)

Before polishing AI Chat, make sure the platform can:

Continuously crawl approved sources
Extract structured athlete information
Build and update athlete dossiers
Detect changes over time
Maintain relationships between entities
Store source citations and confidence scores

This is the core asset you're building.

🥈 Priority 2 — Athlete Dossier

This should become the hero feature.

When someone opens an athlete, they should immediately see:

AI Summary
Timeline
Results
Rankings
Sponsors
Media
Videos
Verified Contacts
Relationship Intelligence
Sources
Confidence

If this page is outstanding, people immediately understand the value.

🥉 Priority 3 — AI Chat

Now AI Chat becomes incredibly powerful because it has something substantial to query.

It should answer questions like:

"Compare these three riders."
"Who has improved the most in the last six months?"
"Show athletes represented by the same manager."
"Which athletes have recently gained sponsors?"
"Which U23 athletes have podium finishes this year?"

The key is that responses are grounded in your structured data plus supporting sources.

4. Intelligence Feed

I actually think this is more valuable than a traditional dashboard.

Imagine opening the platform and seeing:

Today

Athlete A moved up 7 places.
Athlete B announced a new sponsor.
Athlete C won a national championship.
Two new interviews were published.
Three athlete dossiers were updated overnight.

That immediately demonstrates the value of continuous monitoring.

5. Alerts

I agree these are essential for the pilot, but I'd keep them intentionally simple at first:

Ranking changes
Competition results
New media coverage
Sponsorship announcements
Career changes

Let users subscribe to those categories before introducing complex custom rules.

6. Onboarding

This is something I'd actually move much higher than Replit did.

A new user shouldn't land on an empty dashboard.

Instead, guide them through:

Welcome
Create organisation
Add first athlete
Generate first dossier
Follow athlete
Enable alerts
Ask first AI question

That creates an immediate "aha" moment.

7. Schedule

Useful, but I'd keep it later unless your pilot users specifically need calendar views. The same information can initially live within athlete profiles and the dashboard.

8. Stripe

Only after you've got pilot users saying, "I'd pay for this." Until then, it's fine to use manual licences or a simple gated beta.

One thing I think is missing

There's one feature I would add before worrying about polished billing:

Source Explorer

This becomes your trust centre.

Every piece of intelligence should link back to the underlying evidence.

Users should be able to see:

What was found
Where it came from
When it was discovered
Whether it has been verified
Confidence score

For enterprise customers, this is often more important than the AI summary itself because it lets them validate conclusions.

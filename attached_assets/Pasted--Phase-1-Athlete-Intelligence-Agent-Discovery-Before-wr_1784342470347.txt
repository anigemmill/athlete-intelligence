## Product Vision

The product is called **Athlete Intelligence Agent**.

The purpose is to create persistent AI intelligence agents that continuously research, monitor, verify, organise and update publicly available information about athletes.

Unlike ChatGPT or Perplexity, this product is not a search engine.

Users create an Athlete Intelligence Agent once.

That agent continues researching indefinitely.

Instead of searching repeatedly, users receive continuously updated intelligence.

The value is persistent monitoring and actionable insights rather than one-time answers.

---

## Core Problem

Today, scouts, sports organisations, athlete managers, journalists, universities, sponsors and brands spend hours manually researching athletes across dozens of websites.

Information is fragmented across:

* Governing bodies
* Competition results
* Rankings
* News
* Social media
* Podcasts
* Videos
* Interviews
* Sponsorship announcements
* Public databases

The product should automate this research while maintaining trust through source attribution and confidence scoring.

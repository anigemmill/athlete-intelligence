One improvement I'd consider later

Right now you're validating dates in one helper:

isValidDate(...)

That's good.

Long term, though, I think every AI-generated field should go through the same pattern:

AI Output
      ↓
Schema Validation
      ↓
Field Validation
      ↓
Normalisation
      ↓
Database

So eventually you might have validators like:

isValidDate()
isValidCountry()
isValidSocialHandle()
isValidCompetitionLevel()
isValidResult()
isValidTimelineEvent()

At that point, the database layer only ever receives validated data.

That's a larger architectural improvement for a future phase, not something I'd ask Replit to implement now.

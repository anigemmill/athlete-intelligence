UI Visibility & Contrast Fix

The current design contains elements that are technically rendering but are almost invisible due to poor colour contrast.

This is affecting usability and does not match the intended design system.

Issue

In the numbered timeline/process section, the large background numbers ("02", "03", etc.) are almost indistinguishable from the page background.

The divider line is also too subtle.

This section should feel premium and intentional—not hidden.

Audit

Review this entire component and determine:

How the background numbers are styled.
Whether they use opacity, blend modes, filters, or z-index.
Whether the dark theme variables are being applied correctly.
Whether the colour tokens match the new design system.
Whether any parent opacity or overlay is unintentionally reducing visibility.
Fix

Adjust the design so that:

The background numbers are clearly visible while remaining subtle.
The divider line has enough contrast to define the layout.
The section remains elegant and uncluttered.
Contrast meets accessibility guidelines without becoming visually heavy.
All colours come from the central design tokens rather than hard-coded values.

Do not simply increase opacity arbitrarily. Choose colours and opacity that fit the established design language.

Verify

Confirm the section is readable in:

Light mode (if supported)
Dark mode
Standard displays
Lower-brightness displays
High-resolution monitors
Deliverables

Provide:

Root cause of the poor visibility.
Files changed.
Before vs after explanation.
Confirmation that the component now matches the rest of the design system.
